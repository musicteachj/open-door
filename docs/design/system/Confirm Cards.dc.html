1Screen 10: Confirm cards in the conversation (Phase 5)

When someone asks the assistant to book, record, approve, deny, or cancel time off, the assistant answers in one line and Open Door shows a confirm card under it. Every figure on the card comes from the time-off engine. The card's header names the action, its status slot shows the expiry while pending and a status badge once it's a record, and its footer holds the one moment of decision: "Proposed by the AI. Nothing happens until you confirm.", then Confirm and Dismiss. Confirming swaps the footer for a calm apricot line that says what happened; every other ending says "Nothing was changed." and mutes the facts. desktop-pending is live: Confirm, Dismiss, and the rail all respond. The component library is in components.
