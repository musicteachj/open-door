1Screen 04: Directory

The profile is its own page, not a panel beside the list. A page gives every profile a URL that can be shared, and the back link keeps you where you were in the list. The Manager link can lead from profile to profile. Each HR visit to a page is also one clear event for the audit log. A panel would also squeeze the five-column table into about 690 px. Every frame below works: search, filter, open a row, follow the Manager link, go back. 1a is frame 1, and you can switch who it's viewed as with the liveAs setting in Tweaks. 1h scrolls to the end of the first 50 people to show Show more. The data follows the seed company: 120 people, 3 locations and 8 departments, sorted by last name. Marcus's team is the 44 people who report to him directly or through a lead.
