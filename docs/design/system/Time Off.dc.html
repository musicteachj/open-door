The right side is also where the request form opens. The form is a side panel, 420 px wide (the --panel-width token the passage panel uses), docked the same way, so the balances stay in view while Priya picks dates. The page reflows beside it. The date picker opens inline under the Dates field and pushes the rest of the form down, so nothing overlaps the live preview. On a phone the panel becomes a full-width sheet. Every figure comes from one small engine in this file (schedule, holidays, balances, requests), so the frames agree with one another. desktop-default is fully live: open the form, switch type, pick days with the mouse or keyboard, submit, and cancel.
