1Logo round 1: door ajar and one alternative

Both marks keep the 32 px aubergine tile with a 9 px radius, so the lockup, sidebar, and favicon slots don't change. Only two token colors are used: primary #59256A for the tile and surface #F5F7F8 for the light. The tile measures 10.3:1 against surface and 11.1:1 against surface-raised, and the light inside it measures 10.3:1 against the tile. Each mark has its own 16 px drawing aligned to whole pixels, because the 32 px master gets blurry when it's scaled down. The enlarged favicons show both versions side by side. 1a is the lead concept. 1b is the alternative.

The doorway is the light. The door leaf is hinged on the left jamb and swung inward, so it reads as a darker wedge narrowing toward its free edge, with a strip of light showing on the latch side. A thin light jamb and head frame the leaf so it reads separately from the tile. The doorway runs to the bottom edge of the tile, like the current mark, so it still reads as a floor.
