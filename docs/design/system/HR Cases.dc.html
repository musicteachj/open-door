1Screen 12: The HR case queue and the case detail (as Dana Whitfield)

HR's work screens. The queue lists people, categories, dates, and status, never what anyone wrote, sorted only by reply date. The detail shows the AI's summary, labeled as the AI's, and keeps the person's words behind a recorded reveal; the status can't change until the words have been read. Screen 11's lock and quiet tint mark everything confidential. Frames are live: filter the queue, open a row, reveal and hide the words, start a review, close a case. Every time is Mountain time.
