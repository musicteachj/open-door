# TourEndCard

~~~jsx
<TourEndCard persona="Dana" onKeepExploring={close} onChoose={openPersonaMenu} />
~~~

- 520 px, centered over the content sheet. Dana's console stays visible behind it, not dimmed.
- "Keep exploring as Dana" (primary) closes it and ends the tour. "Choose who to be" (secondary) closes it and opens the persona menu.
- No confetti, illustration, animation, or celebration copy.
