1Screen 15: The guided tour

Ten steps, about two minutes, always in a new sandbox that starts as Priya Raman. Each step is a 360 px coach mark beside its target, which gets a 2 px primary ring. There's no overlay, so the page stays usable and the visitor can type their own question. Frames are live: send a chip and it shows "Sent", then "Next" takes the place of "Skip this step"; the X or Escape leaves the tour and shows the toast; "Take the tour" in the menu opens the confirm dialog. Every time is Mountain time. Components are in components.
