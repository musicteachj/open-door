1Screen 11: The case button, the confidential-case form, and My cases

When the safety check routes a message to HR, the panel under the answer offers "Open a confidential case". The form opens inside the panel on a quiet aubergine wash with a lock, never an alert style. The person picks a category, reviews their own words, reads what's true about a case, and sends it. The AI never opens a case. The same form opens from My cases, which lists each case's status and never its words. Every frame is live: open the form, choose, type, send, or press "Not now". The component library is in components.
