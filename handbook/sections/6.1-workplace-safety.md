## Everyone's responsibility

Safety is part of every job at Juniper Row. Everyone, from the stockroom to the office, is expected to work safely, follow site rules, and speak up about hazards. No task is so urgent that it is worth getting hurt.

## Reporting hazards and injuries

Tell your manager right away about a hazard, a near miss, or an injury, no matter how small. If your manager is not available, tell HR. You can raise a safety concern without fear of retaliation, and you do not need proof.

## Warehouse safety

Warehouse staff and forklift operators follow site-specific rules for equipment. Only trained and authorized employees operate forklifts and powered equipment. Wear the protective equipment that your role needs, keep aisles and exits clear, and stack and lift loads the way you were trained. Night shift at the Austin warehouse follows the same rules as day shift.

## Stores and offices

- Keep floors and walkways free of spills and clutter.
- Use ladders and step stools the way they were designed.
- Know where your exits and first aid supplies are.
- Report broken locks, lighting, or equipment to your manager.

## Emergencies

In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell your manager. Each location posts its emergency exits and meeting points. Follow the instructions of the person leading an evacuation.

## Injured at work

If you are hurt at work, get medical help first. Then report the injury to your manager and HR the same day if you can, so that HR can help you with next steps and any time you need away from work. For time away, see [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3).

## Violence and threats

Juniper Row does not allow violence, threats, or intimidation. If you feel unsafe, tell HR or a manager, and call 911 if there is immediate danger. For counseling and support, see [§6.2 Employee Assistance Program](handbook:6.2).
