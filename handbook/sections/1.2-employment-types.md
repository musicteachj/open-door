## The two employment types

Juniper Row has two employment types: salaried and hourly. Your offer letter and your profile in Open Door say which one you are. Several rules in this handbook, including overtime, breaks, and how much paid time off you earn, depend on it.

## Salaried employees

A salaried employee is paid a fixed salary for each pay period, however many hours the work takes in a given week. Salaried roles are mostly office and management roles. Juniper Row classifies salaried roles as exempt from overtime, based on the duties and pay the law requires, which means the overtime rules in [§3.2 Overtime](handbook:3.2) do not apply to them.

## Hourly employees

An hourly employee is paid for every hour worked. Hourly roles include store sales associates, cashiers, customer service, warehouse associates, and forklift operators. Juniper Row classifies hourly roles as non-exempt, which means they are paid overtime for the hours the overtime rules cover. Hourly employees record their time as described in [§3.1 Schedules and timekeeping](handbook:3.1).

## Where the types differ

- Paid time off accrues at 10 hours a month for salaried employees and 8 hours a month for hourly employees. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1).
- Overtime pay and the paid rest break rules apply to hourly employees. See [§3.2 Overtime](handbook:3.2) and [§3.3 Meal and rest breaks](handbook:3.3).
- Sick leave, holidays, bereavement leave, and parental leave work the same way for both types, except where a section says otherwise.

## If your type changes

If you move between salaried and hourly work, HR tells you in writing before the change takes effect. Your paid time off balance moves with you, and nothing you have already earned is lost.
