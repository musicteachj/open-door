## Who you can talk to

You can raise a concern about conduct, safety, pay, fairness, or anything else that worries you with HR directly. You can also talk to your manager, but you are never required to start there, and HR is always an option.

## Ways to raise a concern

1. Open a confidential case in Open Door, from the assistant's HR panel or the My cases page, and send it. Only the HR team sees it. A message to the assistant alone doesn't reach HR.
2. Send an email to the HR team.
3. Ask for a private meeting with someone in HR, in person or by phone.

## What to include

Say what happened, when, where, and who was involved, as much as you know. You do not need proof, and you do not need to name a rule. If you are unsure about some details, say so.

## What happens next

HR acknowledges a concern raised in Open Door within 2 business days. HR then listens to you, decides who else needs to be spoken to, and looks into the matter fairly. HR tells you when the review is complete, and what it can share about the outcome, though some details stay private to protect others.

## If your manager hears a concern

A manager who receives a concern from an employee passes it to HR and does not investigate it or decide the outcome. The manager should tell the employee that HR will follow up.

## Protection from retaliation

Juniper Row forbids retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation. If you think you are being treated worse because you spoke up, raise that with HR too. For how confidential cases are handled, see [§7.2 How confidential HR cases work](handbook:7.2). For harassment and discrimination specifically, see [§2.2 Harassment and discrimination](handbook:2.2).
