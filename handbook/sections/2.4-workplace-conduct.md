## What we expect

Juniper Row expects everyone to treat coworkers, customers, and vendors with courtesy and honesty. Good conduct keeps stores, warehouses, and offices safe and pleasant places to work.

## Showing up and communicating

- Arrive for your scheduled shift on time, and tell your manager as early as you can if you will be late or absent.
- Follow reasonable instructions from your manager and the safety rules for your site.
- Keep communication respectful, whether in person, by phone, or in writing.

## Honesty and company property

Record your time and expenses accurately. Use company equipment, vehicles, and systems for work purposes, and report lost or damaged property promptly. Do not take or use company or customer property without permission.

## Substances and weapons

Do not work while impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs, or any substance that affects your safety. If a prescribed medication may affect your work, talk to HR privately. Do not bring weapons to work, except where the law gives you a right to do so, and tell HR if you think that applies.

## Conflicts of interest

Tell HR if you have an outside job, a business, or a personal relationship that could affect your judgment at work, such as supervising a family member. Telling HR early lets us find a simple fix.

## When conduct falls short

When someone's conduct falls short of these expectations, their manager talks with them, and HR is involved when the matter is serious or repeated. Any corrective step follows a fair process in which the employee can give their side. This section describes that process only. It does not set outcomes, and each situation is decided by people on its facts.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask HR before you act. For concerns about someone else's conduct, see [§2.3 Raising a concern](handbook:2.3).
