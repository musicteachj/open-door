## What a confidential case is

A confidential case is a private conversation with HR about a sensitive concern, such as harassment, discrimination, safety, a medical matter, or a worry about how you are being treated. You can open one in Open Door, and you do not have to tell your manager first.

## Who sees it

Only the HR team sees a case. Managers never see a case, including your own manager. Coworkers do not see it either. HR shares details only with the people who must be involved to look into the matter, and tells you first if that is needed.

## How it is stored

Cases are stored encrypted. Encryption means the case text is scrambled in storage and can be read only through the HR tools that are allowed to open it.

## How fast HR replies

HR replies to a case within 2 business days. A reply may be an answer, a question, or a time to talk. If your concern involves immediate danger, call 911 first, and do not wait for a reply.

## The role of the AI

Open Door's assistant is an AI. It reads your message so that it can recognize a sensitive topic and send it to a person. When a case is opened, the AI writes HR a short, neutral summary of what you wrote, with no recommendation about what HR should do. The AI never decides anything about a case. People make every decision.

## What the AI does not do

The AI does not judge whether you are right, rank the seriousness of a concern, or suggest discipline, termination, or ratings for anyone. It does not reply to you about the merits of your case. HR does.

## No retaliation

Juniper Row forbids retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation. See [§2.3 Raising a concern](handbook:2.3) and [§2.2 Harassment and discrimination](handbook:2.2).

## Support while you wait

You do not have to wait alone. The Employee Assistance Program offers free and confidential counseling at any hour. See [§6.2 Employee Assistance Program](handbook:6.2).
