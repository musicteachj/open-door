## What Juniper Row holds

Juniper Row keeps a record about you so that it can employ and pay you. The record holds your work contact details, your job and location, your manager, your employment type, your pay, and your time off, including requests, balances, and approvals.

## Why we keep it

We use this information to run payroll, schedule work, manage time off, and meet our duties as an employer. We do not use it for anything unrelated to your employment.

## Who can see what

- **Coworkers** can see work details in the company directory, such as your name, job title, location, and work contact details.
- **Your manager** can see work and time-off details for the people who report to them, such as schedules, leave requests, and balances.
- **HR** can see more, including pay and employment records, because HR needs them to do its job.

Your manager cannot see HR's confidential cases, and coworkers cannot see your pay or your time-off balance.

## Sensitive information

Medical information, accommodation requests, and confidential cases are held separately and shared only with the people who need them. See [§5.3 Accommodations](handbook:5.3) and [§7.2 How confidential HR cases work](handbook:7.2).

## Asking for a copy

You can ask HR for a copy of your record. HR sends you the information it holds about you, such as your job details, pay, and time off. Ask HR in Open Door or by email.

## Correcting your information

Tell HR if something in your record is wrong or out of date, such as your name, contact details, or the account where your pay is deposited. HR corrects the record and confirms the change with you. See [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4) for how pay is deposited.

## Keeping it safe

Only people with a work reason can open your record, and access is tracked. If you think someone has seen your information without a reason, tell HR.
