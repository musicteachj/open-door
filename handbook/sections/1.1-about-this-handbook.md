## What this handbook is

The Juniper Row Employee Handbook explains how work, pay, time off, and support work at Juniper Row. Juniper Row is a fictional retailer, and this handbook is demo content written for Open Door. It is not legal advice, and nothing in it describes a real employer.

## Who it covers

This handbook applies to everyone at Juniper Row: about 120 people at the Denver HQ in Colorado, the Oakland store in California, and the Austin warehouse in Texas. Some sections apply only to hourly employees or only to certain locations, and those sections say so at the top.

## Company sections and state addenda

The company sections set the rules that apply to everyone. A state addendum is a short extra section for a state whose law is different from the company rule. Addendum sections have an ID that starts with the state code, such as CA-4.3 for California sick leave.

## Which rule applies to you

If a state addendum applies to you and it differs from the company section, you receive whichever rule is more generous to you. You never lose a benefit because a company section and an addendum say different things.

## Finding answers

Every section has a number, a title, and a "last updated" date. Open Door's assistant answers policy questions from these sections and cites the section it used, so you can open the passage and read it yourself. If the handbook does not answer your question, the assistant says so and offers to send the question to HR.

## Keeping it current

HR reviews the handbook at least once a year. When a section changes, its "last updated" date changes with it. For the rules on employment types, see [§1.2 Employment types](handbook:1.2).
