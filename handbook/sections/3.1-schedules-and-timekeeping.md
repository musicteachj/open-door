## The workweek

The Juniper Row workweek runs from Sunday at 12:00 a.m. to Saturday at 11:59 p.m. Hours, overtime, and pay periods are all counted by this workweek, at every location.

## Your schedule

Your manager sets your schedule and shares it in advance. Store and warehouse schedules can change with customer demand, and your manager tells you about a change as early as possible. The Austin warehouse runs day and night shifts, and your shift is part of your schedule.

## Your schedule in Open Door

Your schedule in Open Door shows the days of the week you normally work. For time off, each scheduled day counts as 8 hours. Time off counts only your scheduled days, and never a company holiday.

## Recording time if you are hourly

Hourly employees record their time in the timekeeping system. Clock in when your shift starts and clock out when it ends, and record your meal break as described in [§3.3 Meal and rest breaks](handbook:3.3). If you forget to clock in or out, tell your manager the same day so the record can be corrected.

## All time worked is paid

Juniper Row pays hourly employees for all time worked. That includes time spent on tasks before or after a shift, such as opening, closing, or handing off to the next shift. Off-the-clock work is not allowed. If a manager asks you to work off the clock, say no and tell HR.

## Checking your time

Look at your recorded hours before each payday. If something looks wrong, tell your manager or HR right away. Corrections are made on the next payday at the latest, and any missed pay is added.

## Salaried employees

Salaried employees do not clock in and out. They are expected to work the hours their role needs and to let their manager know when they are out. See [§1.2 Employment types](handbook:1.2) for the difference between the two types.
