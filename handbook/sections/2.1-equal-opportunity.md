## Our commitment

Juniper Row gives every applicant and employee a fair chance. We make decisions about hiring, pay, scheduling, promotion, training, and leaving based on the work and the role, not on who a person is. This applies at every location and to every employment type.

## What we do not allow

Juniper Row does not allow decisions or treatment based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, military or veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law where you work.

## How it shows up in daily work

- Job postings describe the real requirements of the role.
- Managers apply the same standards to everyone in the same role.
- Pay, schedules, and opportunities are reviewed for fairness by HR.
- Training and promotion are open to everyone who meets the stated requirements.

## Help with accommodations

If you need a change at work because of a disability, pregnancy, or a religious practice, Juniper Row works with you to find one that fits. The process is in [§5.3 Accommodations](handbook:5.3).

## If you see something unfair

If you think you or a coworker has been treated unfairly, tell HR. You can do it in Open Door, by email, or in person, and you do not have to tell your manager first. Juniper Row does not allow retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. See [§2.3 Raising a concern](handbook:2.3) for how to report and what happens next.

## Where managers fit

Managers help carry out this policy. A manager who learns that someone may have been treated unfairly passes it to HR and does not try to settle it alone.
