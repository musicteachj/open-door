## What the program is

The Juniper Row Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free and confidential short-term counseling for employees and the members of their households. It is run by an outside counseling service, separate from Juniper Row.

## What it can help with

You can use the EAP for stress, anxiety, grief, relationship or family concerns, money worries, substance use, or any other concern that is weighing on you. You do not need to be in crisis, and you do not need to explain your reasons to anyone at work.

## How much it offers

The EAP provides up to 6 sessions per issue each year, at no cost to you or your household members. Sessions can be by phone or video, depending on the counselor.

## How to reach it

Call the EAP at 1-800-555-0137. The line is available 24 hours a day. A counselor helps you choose the right kind of support and schedule your first session.

## Your privacy

Your use of the EAP is confidential. Juniper Row never learns who uses it. The counseling service reports only combined numbers to Juniper Row, and never names or details.

## If you are in crisis

If you are thinking about harming yourself, or are worried about someone who is, call or text 988, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or chat through the 988 Lifeline's website. It is available 24 hours a day, every day, and it is free and confidential. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

## Time for appointments

You can ask your manager for time to attend sessions, and you do not need to say why. You can use sick leave for counseling that is care for your health. See [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3).

## Other support

For grief after a death, see [§4.4 Bereavement leave](handbook:4.4). For a workplace concern, see [§2.3 Raising a concern](handbook:2.3).
