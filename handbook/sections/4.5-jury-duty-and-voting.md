## Jury duty pay

Juniper Row pays your full regular pay for up to 10 working days of jury service in a year. For hourly employees, full regular pay means pay for your scheduled hours. Jury duty pay is separate from PTO, so it does not reduce your balance.

## Juror fees

You keep any fee or mileage payment the court gives you. Juniper Row does not deduct it from your pay.

## What to do

Bring your summons to your manager as soon as you receive it, and tell your manager when you are scheduled to serve. If your service will last longer than 10 working days, talk to HR about options such as unpaid leave or using PTO.

## Returning to work

If the court releases you early in the day, contact your manager about whether to return for the rest of your shift. When your service ends, tell your manager and return to your regular schedule.

## Time to vote

If you do not have at least 3 consecutive hours outside your working hours while the polls are open, you can take up to 2 hours of paid time off to vote. Ask your manager before Election Day, and at least 2 working days ahead when you can, so your manager can plan coverage. The time is paid and does not reduce your PTO.

## Texas rules

The Texas addendum covers the rules that apply to Texas employees. See [§TX-4.5 Texas jury duty and voting](handbook:TX-4.5). Where a state rule is more generous to you, it applies.

## Questions

Ask HR if you are unsure how to count your days of jury service, or if you are called for service more than once in a year.
