## How much you receive

Juniper Row gives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave to any parent after a birth, an adoption, or a foster placement. "Any parent" includes birth parents, adoptive parents, foster parents, and partners, regardless of gender.

## Who qualifies

You qualify after 6 months of employment at Juniper Row. You must take the leave within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## How the pay works

You are paid your regular pay during parental leave, for your scheduled hours if you are hourly. Parental leave pay is separate from PTO and sick leave, so taking it does not reduce either. Your health coverage continues on the same terms as when you were working.

## Taking the leave

You can take the leave all at once or in shorter blocks within the 12 months, if you agree on a plan with your manager and HR. Tell HR as early as you can, ideally at least 30 days before the leave begins, so coverage can be planned. If the date of an adoption or placement is uncertain, tell HR what you know and update HR when plans change.

## Using it with family and medical leave

Parental leave can run at the same time as the leave in [§5.1 Family and medical leave](handbook:5.1). When they run together, you receive full pay for the 6 weeks, and the rest of your leave under 5.1 is unpaid, unless you choose to use PTO or sick leave.

## Returning to work

HR talks with you before you return about your schedule. If you need changes to your work after pregnancy or because of a medical condition, see [§5.3 Accommodations](handbook:5.3). If you are nursing when you return, see [§5.4 Lactation](handbook:5.4).

## State rules

A state may give additional paid family leave. Where a state rule is more generous to you, it applies. Ask HR how a state program works alongside this benefit.
