## How much you receive

Paid time off (PTO) is time away from work that you are paid for, to use for any reason. Salaried employees earn 10 hours of PTO per month, which is 120 hours a year. Hourly employees earn 8 hours of PTO per month, which is 96 hours a year.

## When you earn it

You earn PTO on the last day of each month you work. You can use it as soon as it is earned. PTO is separate from sick leave and from holidays, so using one never reduces the others. See [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3) and [§4.2 Holidays](handbook:4.2).

## When you can use it

You can use PTO for any reason: a vacation, an errand, a personal day, or rest. You do not have to explain why. Request it in Open Door, and your manager reviews the request.

## Giving notice

Give your manager notice for time off. Where you can, give at least two weeks' notice for more than three days off. For shorter time away, give as much notice as you reasonably can. If something unexpected comes up, tell your manager as soon as possible.

## The balance ceiling

Your PTO balance can grow to 1.5 times your yearly amount. That is 180 hours for salaried employees and 144 hours for hourly employees. When your balance is at the ceiling, you stop earning PTO until you use some, and then you start earning again.

## Nothing is ever taken away

Juniper Row never takes away PTO you have earned. Unused PTO carries into the next year, and there is no use-it-or-lose-it deadline. The ceiling only pauses new earning. It never reduces your balance.

## When employment ends

Unused PTO is paid out when your employment ends, whatever the reason. It is included in your final pay. See [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4).
