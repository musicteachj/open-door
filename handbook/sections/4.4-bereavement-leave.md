## Paid time after a death

Juniper Row gives paid bereavement leave so that you can grieve, make arrangements, and be with family. Bereavement leave is separate from PTO and sick leave, so using it does not reduce either.

## Close family members

You receive up to 5 paid days for the death of a close family member. Close family members are your:

- spouse or domestic partner
- child
- parent
- sibling
- grandparent
- grandchild
- parent-in-law

## Other relatives

You receive 2 paid days for the death of another relative, such as an aunt, uncle, cousin, niece, or nephew, or a step-relative or in-law not listed above.

## When you can take the days

The days do not need to be consecutive, and you can use them within three months of the death. This lets you attend a service or gathering that happens later, or travel when you can.

## Hourly employees

Hourly employees are paid for their scheduled hours on each bereavement day. If you were not scheduled to work on a day, that day does not count against your bereavement leave.

## How to ask

Tell your manager as soon as you can, and record the days in Open Door. You do not need to give details, and HR may ask only for the date of the death and your relationship to the person.

## If you need more time

If you need more time than bereavement leave gives you, you can use PTO or sick leave where it applies. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1) and [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3). For counseling, the Employee Assistance Program is free and confidential. See [§6.2 Employee Assistance Program](handbook:6.2).
