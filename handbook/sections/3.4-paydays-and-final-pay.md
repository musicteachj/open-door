## When you are paid

Juniper Row pays every other Friday, which is biweekly. Each payday covers the two workweeks that ended on the Saturday before it. For example, a payday on a Friday pays for the two Sunday-to-Saturday workweeks that finished six days earlier. See [§3.1 Schedules and timekeeping](handbook:3.1) for the workweek.

## How you are paid

Pay is deposited directly into the account you choose. Your pay statement shows your hours, your pay, and deductions. Salaried employees receive their fixed salary each pay period, and hourly employees receive pay for every hour recorded.

## Errors in your pay

If you think your pay is wrong, tell your manager or HR right away. HR checks the record. If you were underpaid, the difference is paid no later than the next payday. If you were overpaid, HR talks to you before taking any step.

## Final pay

When your employment ends, for any reason, Juniper Row pays your final wages, including any unused paid time off, no later than the next regular payday. Unused PTO is paid out whatever the reason you leave. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1). Unused sick leave is not paid out.

## State rules on final pay

Some states require final pay sooner than the next regular payday. If you work in California, Colorado, or Texas, see [CA-3.4 California final pay](handbook:CA-3.4), [CO-3.4 Colorado final pay](handbook:CO-3.4), or [TX-3.4 Texas final pay](handbook:TX-3.4). Where a state rule is more generous to you, it applies.

## Before you leave

Return company property and confirm where to send your final pay statement. HR sends you a summary of your final pay and your paid time off payout.
