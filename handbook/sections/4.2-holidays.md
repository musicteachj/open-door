## The six company holidays

Every Juniper Row location closes on six holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day (the last Monday in May), Independence Day, Labor Day (the first Monday in September), Thanksgiving (the fourth Thursday in November), and Christmas Day.

## When a holiday falls on a weekend

A holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before. A holiday that falls on a Sunday is observed on the Monday after. The closing day you see on the company calendar is the observed date.

## New Year's Day on a Saturday

When New Year's Day falls on a Saturday, every location closes on Friday, December 31 of the year before. For example, New Year's Day 2028 is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

## Denver HQ closes one more day

Denver HQ also closes the day after Thanksgiving, in addition to the six holidays that every location observes. The Oakland store and the Austin warehouse do not close that day.

## Holidays do not use PTO

You do not use paid time off for a company holiday. Your PTO balance stays the same, and holidays are separate from the time you earn under [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1).

## Holiday pay for hourly employees

Hourly employees receive 8 hours of holiday pay, at their regular hourly rate, for each company holiday their location is closed. Salaried employees receive their regular pay.

## Questions about the calendar

The company calendar lists the closing dates for your location. If you are unsure whether your location is closed on a given day, ask your manager or HR.
