This addendum applies to employees working in Texas, including everyone at the Austin warehouse. It adds to [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4). Where the Texas rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## When final pay is due

Texas sets the deadline by how your employment ends:

- If you are laid off, discharged, or otherwise involuntarily separated, your final pay is due within 6 calendar days.
- If you quit, retire, or resign, your final pay is due on the next regularly scheduled payday after your last day.

The company rule is the next regular payday in every case. When your employment ends involuntarily, the next regular payday may come later than 6 calendar days. In that case the Texas deadline is sooner, so it is the one that applies to you.

## Final pay cannot be held back

Juniper Row cannot hold your final pay back past the deadline for reasons such as unreturned company property. If you still have company property, HR works with you on returning it, and your pay is not delayed while that happens.

## Unused PTO is included

Your final pay includes your unused paid time off under Juniper Row's policy. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1). Unused sick leave is not paid out, as described in [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3).

## Questions

If you think your final pay is wrong or late, tell HR right away. HR checks the record and corrects any underpayment.
