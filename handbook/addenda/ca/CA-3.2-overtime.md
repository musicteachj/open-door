This addendum applies to hourly employees working in California, including everyone at the Oakland store. It adds to [§3.2 Overtime](handbook:3.2). Where the California rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## Daily overtime

California counts overtime by the day as well as by the week. You are paid at time and one-half your regular rate for:

- all hours over 8 in a workday, up to and including 12 hours
- all hours over 40 in a workweek
- the first 8 hours worked on the seventh consecutive day of work in a workweek

## Double time

You are paid at double your regular rate for:

- all hours over 12 in a workday
- all hours over 8 on the seventh consecutive day of work in a workweek

## How this fits the company rule

The company section pays overtime for hours over 40 in a workweek. In California, the daily and seventh-day rules above come on top of it, so you can earn overtime in a week that totals fewer than 40 hours. Hours are never paid twice. The higher rate for a given hour is the one that applies.

The workweek is Sunday through Saturday, as described in [§3.1 Schedules and timekeeping](handbook:3.1).

## If your pay looks wrong

If a pay statement does not show daily overtime or double time you believe you earned, tell your manager or HR. HR checks your recorded hours and corrects any underpayment, as described in [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4).
