This addendum applies to employees working in California, including everyone at the Oakland store. It adds to [§5.4 Lactation](handbook:5.4). Where the California rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## Your right to ask

California law gives you the right to request lactation accommodation. You ask the way [§5.4 Lactation](handbook:5.4) describes: tell your manager or HR, with no doctor's note. If Juniper Row can't provide break time or a space that meets the policy, HR gives you a written response.

## Extra pay if a break or a space is denied

If you are denied reasonable break time or an adequate space to express milk, you are owed one additional hour of pay at your regular rate for each violation. Tell HR, and HR corrects your pay. See [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4).

## Complaints to the Labor Commissioner

You have the right to file a complaint with the California Labor Commissioner for any violation of your lactation rights. If you are penalized for asking for or using lactation breaks, you can also file a retaliation complaint with the Labor Commissioner within one year.

## Getting a copy of this policy

HR gives this policy to every new California employee when they are hired, and to anyone who asks about or requests parental leave. See [§5.2 Parental leave](handbook:5.2).
