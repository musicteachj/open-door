This addendum applies to employees working in California, including everyone at the Oakland store. It adds to [§5.1 Family and medical leave](handbook:5.1). Where the California rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## The California Family Rights Act

The California Family Rights Act (CFRA) covers employers with 5 or more employees, so it covers Juniper Row. It has no requirement of 50 employees within 75 miles. That means Oakland store employees can qualify for CFRA leave even though, as [§5.1 Family and medical leave](handbook:5.1) explains, the federal law likely does not reach them.

You are eligible if you have worked for Juniper Row for more than 12 months and for at least 1,250 hours in the 12 months before your leave starts.

## Reasons for leave

You can take up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave for:

- your own serious health condition
- caring for a child, spouse, domestic partner, parent, parent-in-law, grandparent, grandchild, sibling, or designated person who has a serious health condition
- bonding with a new child by birth, adoption, or foster placement
- certain military family needs

Juniper Row may limit leave for a designated person to one designated person per 12-month period.

## Job and health coverage

You return to the same or a comparable position. Your health coverage continues during leave.

## Pay

CFRA leave is unpaid. You may be able to get partial wage replacement from California's Paid Family Leave or State Disability Insurance programs, which the state Employment Development Department runs. You may also use PTO or sick leave. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1) and [§CA-4.3 California sick leave](handbook:CA-4.3).

## Notice and certification

Give reasonable advance notice when you can plan the leave, and tell HR as soon as practicable when you cannot. Juniper Row may ask for a health care provider's certification.

## How it fits the company leave

Juniper Row's own leave in [§5.1 Family and medical leave](handbook:5.1) and CFRA leave run at the same time. They do not add together.
