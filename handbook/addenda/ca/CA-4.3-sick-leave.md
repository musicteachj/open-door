This addendum applies to employees working in California, including everyone at the Oakland store. It adds to [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3). Where the California rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## How much you receive

In California you receive at least 40 hours, or 5 of your workdays, of paid sick leave each year, whichever is more. You receive all of it on January 1. This is called the up-front method.

A new hire receives the full amount on their first day and can use it right away. Juniper Row does not make you wait before you use it.

## What you can use it for

You can use sick leave for:

- diagnosis, care, or treatment of an existing health condition, for you or a family member
- preventive care, for you or a family member
- serving on a jury, or appearing in court as a witness under a subpoena or court order
- reasons related to being a victim of a qualifying act of violence, such as domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking, for you or a family member

A family member is your parent, child, spouse, registered domestic partner, grandparent, grandchild, or sibling, or a person you designate.

## Notes and notice

Juniper Row does not ask for a doctor's note to use sick leave in California, so the note rule in the company section does not apply to you. When you know about the need ahead of time, give your manager advance notice. When you do not, tell your manager as soon as practical.

## What happens to unused hours

Because you receive the full amount at the start of each year, unused hours do not carry over. The new amount replaces them. Unused sick leave is not paid out when your employment ends. If you are rehired within 12 months, your unused sick leave is restored.

## Longer illness

If you need more time than your sick leave covers, see [CA-5.1 California family and medical leave](handbook:CA-5.1).
