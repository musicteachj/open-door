This addendum applies to hourly employees working in California, including everyone at the Oakland store. It adds to [§3.3 Meal and rest breaks](handbook:3.3). Where the California rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## Meal periods

You receive a 30-minute meal period when you work more than 5 hours in a day. Juniper Row's company rule already meets this. California adds a second 30-minute meal period when you work more than 10 hours in a day.

You and Juniper Row may agree to skip a meal period in two cases:

- The first meal period may be skipped by mutual consent only if your workday is no more than 6 hours.
- The second meal period may be skipped by mutual consent only if your workday is no more than 12 hours and you did not skip the first.

A meal period is unpaid only when you are relieved of all duty. If you are not relieved of all duty, the time is paid work time.

## Rest periods

You receive a paid, uninterrupted rest period of a net 10 minutes for every 4 hours you work, or major fraction of 4 hours. If your total daily work time is under 3.5 hours, no rest period is required. Rest periods are paid, so you do not clock out for them.

## The premium for a missed break

If a meal period is not provided as described above, you are owed one additional hour of pay at your regular rate for that workday. If a rest period is not provided, you are owed one additional hour of pay at your regular rate for that workday.

## Report a missed break

If you did not get a meal or rest period, tell your manager or HR so the premium can be paid. HR reviews each report and corrects your pay on the next payday. See [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4).
