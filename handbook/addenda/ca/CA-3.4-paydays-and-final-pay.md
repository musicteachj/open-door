This addendum applies to employees working in California, including everyone at the Oakland store. It adds to [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4). Where the California rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## When final pay is due

California sets the deadline by how your employment ends:

- If Juniper Row discharges you, all your wages are due immediately, at the time your employment ends.
- If you quit, give at least 72 hours' notice, and leave on the day you named, all your wages are due on your last day.
- If you quit without 72 hours' notice, all your wages are due within 72 hours.

These deadlines replace the company rule of the next regular payday. They are sooner, so they are the ones that apply to you.

## Unused PTO is included

Your final wages include all of your unused paid time off. California treats PTO the same as vacation, so it is earned wages and is paid out whatever the reason you leave. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1).

Unused sick leave is a separate benefit and is not paid out. See [§CA-4.3 California sick leave](handbook:CA-4.3).

## Planning your last day

If you plan to leave, tell your manager and HR as early as you can. Giving at least 72 hours' notice means your final pay is ready on your last day. HR prepares your final wages ahead of time when it knows the date.

## Questions

If you think your final pay is wrong or late, tell HR right away. HR checks the record and corrects any underpayment.
