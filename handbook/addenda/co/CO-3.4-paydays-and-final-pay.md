This addendum applies to employees working in Colorado, including everyone at Denver HQ. It adds to [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4). Where the Colorado rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## When final pay is due

Colorado sets the deadline by how your employment ends:

- If Juniper Row ends your employment, your final wages are due immediately.
- If the payroll office is not open at that time, your final wages are available no later than six hours after the start of its next regular workday. If the payroll office is off site, the limit is 24 hours.
- If you quit or resign, your final wages are due on the next regular payday.

When Juniper Row ends your employment, these deadlines are sooner than the company rule of the next regular payday, so they are the ones that apply to you. When you resign, the company rule and the Colorado rule are the same.

## Unused PTO is always paid

Your final wages include all of your unused paid time off, whatever the reason you leave. That is true even if you resign without notice or your employment ends for cause. Earned PTO cannot be forfeited. See [§4.1 Paid time off](handbook:4.1).

Unused sick leave is a separate benefit and is not paid out. See [§CO-4.3 Colorado sick leave](handbook:CO-4.3).

## Questions

If you think your final pay is wrong or late, tell HR right away. HR checks the record and corrects any underpayment.
