This addendum applies to employees working in Colorado, including everyone at Denver HQ. It adds to [§4.3 Sick leave](handbook:4.3). Where the Colorado rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## How much you receive

Colorado's Healthy Families and Workplaces Act requires 1 hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours a year, from your first day of work. Juniper Row simplifies this: in Colorado you have **48 hours** available on January 1. A new hire has 48 hours on their first day and can use it right away.

## Carryover

Unused hours carry over. On January 1, your balance is raised back to 48 hours rather than reset, so you never start a year with less than 48. You can use up to 48 hours a year.

## What you can use it for

You can use sick leave for:

- your own mental or physical illness, injury, or health condition
- preventive care or medical diagnosis, care, or treatment, for you or a family member
- caring for a family member who needs the same
- needs related to domestic abuse, sexual assault, or criminal harassment, such as medical care, counseling, victim services, legal services, or relocation
- a public health emergency that closes your workplace or your child's school or place of care
- grieving, attending a funeral or memorial, or handling financial or legal needs after a family member's death
- evacuating your home, or caring for a family member whose school or place of care closed, because of bad weather, loss of power, heat, or water, or another unexpected event

A family member is immediate family related by blood, adoption, marriage, or civil union, or anyone you are responsible for providing or arranging health- or safety-related care for.

## Documentation and notice

Juniper Row can ask for reasonable documentation only for an absence of 4 or more consecutive workdays that you would normally have worked. It is asked for after you return, not before you take the leave. Your own written statement is acceptable if you did not see a provider or cannot get a provider's note in time or without cost. You never have to describe your condition. Tell your manager as soon as practicable when the need is foreseeable.

## Protections

Using sick leave never counts as an absence that leads to discipline, and you are never asked to find a replacement. Unused sick leave is not paid out when your employment ends.
