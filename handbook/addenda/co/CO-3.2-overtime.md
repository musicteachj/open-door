This addendum applies to hourly employees working in Colorado, including everyone at Denver HQ. It adds to [§3.2 Overtime](handbook:3.2). Where the Colorado rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## When overtime is owed

Colorado pays overtime at time and one-half your regular rate for any of these:

- hours over 40 in a workweek
- hours over 12 in a workday
- hours over any 12 consecutive hours

If more than one of these applies to the same hours, you are paid whichever gives you more pay. The same hour is never paid at overtime twice.

## No comp time, no averaging

Juniper Row cannot give you time off instead of overtime pay. This is sometimes called comp time. Overtime is paid in money. Hours also cannot be averaged across weeks, so a short week does not cancel overtime you earned in a long one.

## How this fits the company rule

The company section pays overtime for hours over 40 in a workweek. In Colorado, the daily and consecutive-hour rules above come on top of it, so you can earn overtime in a week that totals fewer than 40 hours.

The workweek is Sunday through Saturday, as described in [§3.1 Schedules and timekeeping](handbook:3.1).

## If your pay looks wrong

If a pay statement does not show overtime you believe you earned, tell your manager or HR. HR checks your recorded hours and corrects any underpayment, as described in [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4).
