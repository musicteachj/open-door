This addendum applies to hourly employees working in Colorado, including everyone at Denver HQ. It adds to [§3.3 Meal and rest breaks](handbook:3.3). Where the Colorado rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## Meal periods

For shifts over 5 hours, you receive an uninterrupted, duty-free 30-minute meal period. It is unpaid only if you are completely relieved of duty and free for personal activity. If the work makes that impractical, you may eat on duty, and that time is paid. As far as practical, your meal period falls at least 1 hour after your shift starts and 1 hour before it ends.

## Rest periods

You receive a paid 10-minute rest period for each 4 hours of work. The number of rest periods depends on the length of your shift:

- 2 hours or fewer: none
- over 2 hours up to 6 hours: 1
- over 6 hours up to 10 hours: 2
- over 10 hours up to 14 hours: 3
- each further 4 hours: 1 more

Where practical, your rest period falls in the middle of each 4-hour period. A rest period must not include any work, and you do not have to leave the premises.

## If a rest period is missed

If a required rest period is not authorized and permitted, extra pay is owed to you for that time. Tell your manager or HR if you did not get a rest period, so the pay can be corrected. See [§3.4 Paydays and final pay](handbook:3.4).
