This addendum applies to employees working in Colorado, including everyone at Denver HQ. It adds to [§5.1 Family and medical leave](handbook:5.1). Where the Colorado rule or the company rule is more generous to you, the more generous one applies.

## Colorado's paid leave program

Colorado's Family and Medical Leave Insurance program, called FAMLI, is run by the state. It provides up to 12 weeks of paid leave in a 12-month period so you can:

- recover from your own serious health condition
- care for a family member or loved one
- seek safety from domestic violence or assault
- bond with a new child, including an adopted or fostered child
- prepare for a family member's military deployment

Birthing parents with pregnancy or childbirth complications may get up to 4 additional weeks. Parents of an infant in neonatal intensive care may get up to 12 additional weeks.

## Pay and eligibility

FAMLI benefits are partial wage replacement, up to 90% of your average weekly wage, with lower earners receiving a higher percentage, up to a weekly maximum the state sets. Most workers qualify: you are eligible if you have earned at least $2,500 in Colorado wages in the previous five quarters.

## Job and health coverage

If you have been employed at Juniper Row for at least 180 calendar days before your leave starts, you return to your job or an equivalent position with equivalent pay and benefits. Your health coverage continues during leave. Retaliation and interference are prohibited.

## How to apply

You apply to the state FAMLI Division, not to Juniper Row. You can file a planned leave up to 30 days before it starts. Juniper Row will not require you to use PTO or sick leave before or during FAMLI leave.

## How it fits the company leave

When the reason qualifies for both, FAMLI leave and Juniper Row's own leave in [§5.1 Family and medical leave](handbook:5.1) run at the same time. For sick leave, see [§CO-4.3 Colorado sick leave](handbook:CO-4.3).
